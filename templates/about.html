Here's how it works: every 10 minutes, a cron job triggers a series of Python scripts that scrape, dedupe, and collate the latest 25 news stories from a curated collection of RSS feeds and public APIs. Another Python script transforms that scraped data into a small (~500K) payload of pure HTML and CSS and uploads it to a server.
