About bandwidth

bandwidth is a text-based, bandwidth-friendly news aggregator. The site pulls content from public RSS feeds and open source news projects, then regenerates every 10 minutes.

What we don't do

  • No JavaScript
  • No images
  • No cookies
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No advertising

What we do

  • Plain text news that loads instantly
  • Content from trusted sources
  • Updates every 10 minutes
  • Works on any device, any connection

Funding

bandwidth is 100% audience-funded. We don't sell advertising space to corporations. If you find this useful, please consider supporting us.
