Bandwidth is a text-based aggregator for non-profit and open-source news. No client-side JavaScript, images, cookies, or tracking pixels to be found. WYSIWYG.

Here's how it works: every 10 minutes, a cron job triggers a series of Python scripts that scrape, dedupe, and collate the latest 25 news stories from a curated collection of RSS feeds and public APIs:

  • ProPublica
  • The Markup
  • The Intercept
  • Center for Public Integrity
  • Global Voices
  • Truthout
  • Reveal
  • Grist
  • Inside Climate News
  • Bellingcat
  • Wikinews
  • Nonprofit Quarterly

Another Python script transforms that scraped data into a small (<500K) payload of pure HTML and CSS and uploads it to a server.
